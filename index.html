    No. Velmio is a service. You tell us about your business and our team prepares the video for you.

    Do I need professional photos?

    No. Phone photos are fine. Our team works with what you send.

    Can I use my existing business website?

    Yes. Share your website and our team will use it to understand your business.

    Can I upload my own logo and product images?

    Yes. You can add your logo, business photos, and product images when you order.

    Which social media platforms are supported?

    Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

    How long will it take to make a video?

    Within 24 hours of your order. We'll email you as soon as it's ready.

    Yes. Each video includes 1 round of changes. Just reply to your delivery email.

    A vertical 9:16, high-resolution MP4 file that's ready to post.

    Yes. You own it, so you can run it as an ad under each platform's rules.

    No. Our team does all the editing for you.

    We use the details, website, and images you send us, and we may use AI tools to help review them.

    Yes. Your script and voiceover are prepared for you.

    You pay when you choose your package, after you share your business details.
