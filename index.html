Questions, Answered.

How do I make a video?

Sign up, add your photos and logo, describe what the video should show, and pick a size. Velmio makes the video right in your browser, and you can download it when it's ready.

Do I pay anything when I sign up?

No. Signing up is free and doesn't need a payment method. You only pay if you choose a paid plan.

How long will it take to make a video?

Right now, about as long as the video itself, which is usually under a minute. Once your photos and script are ready, Velmio renders the video while you watch.

How many photos will I need?

At least 3, and six works best. Phone photos are fine, and you can add your logo if you have one. Your photos stay on your device.

Do I need editing experience?

No. You add your photos and describe what should happen, and Velmio handles the motion, transitions, and captions. You can edit any line of the script before you make the video.

Does the video include a voiceover?

You can pick a voice from your device to hear your script during the preview, but that voice isn't in the downloaded video. Studio voices, where available, are included in the download.

Can I use the video for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook?

Yes. You can make vertical, portrait, square, or wide videos and post them from your own accounts, under each platform's rules. Use Chrome, Edge, or Safari to save an MP4 file.

Can I change the video after it's created?

Yes. Choose Edit Video, change your script, voice, or size, and make a new version.

Who owns the videos I make?

You do. You keep the rights to your photos, your logo, and the videos you make with them.

Can I cancel a monthly subscription?

Yes. You can cancel any time, and your plan ends at the next renewal. You won't be charged again after that.

Can I get a refund?

Paid videos and monthly plans can't be refunded once delivered, except where the law requires it.
